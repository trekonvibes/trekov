Privacy

Last updated 14 September 2026

Trekov is a travel app: a map, your trips, and — when you ride as a group — everyone on one screen while you move. This page says exactly what it keeps, what other people can see, and how to get rid of all of it.

The short version. Your live location is never stored on our servers — it goes straight to the people on your trip while you are navigating, and stops when you do. We do not sell location data, we never read your contacts, and there are no advertising trackers in the app.

Live location and voice

While you are navigating a group trip with sharing switched on, your position is sent directly to the other riders on that trip through a private channel. It is not written to a database, not kept in a history, and not visible to anyone who is not on the trip. Push-to-talk audio travels between riders' phones and is never recorded.

  • The master switch — turn sharing off and nothing about your position leaves the phone. You still see everyone else.
  • Per person — you can hide from one rider while the rest of the group still sees you. That rider's app is told not to show you; it cannot be fully enforced on someone else's phone, so switch sharing off if it matters.
  • Close the trip, and the sharing stops.

Who can see what

  • Public: your handle, name, bio, profile photo and the photos you post.
  • The trip only: a trip's stops, your notes, bookings, live positions and voice — the host and the riders who have accepted, nobody else.
  • An open ride: if a host marks a group trip public, other riders can find it in a list. They see only the title, the dates, where it starts and ends, how many are going and the host's handle — never the notes, the bookings or anyone's position. The host approves each rider before they see any more.
  • Nobody but you: your email address, your saved places and your device id.

Companies that handle your data for us

  • Supabase — the database, sign-in, photo storage and the private channels group trips use.
  • Google Maps Platform — the map, searching for places, place details and directions. Your search terms and the map area you are looking at reach Google as part of that.
  • Google Navigation SDK (Android app) — turn-by-turn navigation. While you navigate, your location, route and destination go to Google to calculate the route, follow traffic and guide you, under Google's own terms, which you accept the first time you navigate.
  • OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap — the map tiles Trekov downloads for offline use.
  • Project OSRM — route calculation.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — delivering notifications to your phone.
  • Razorpay — payments, if and when paid plans are switched on. Card details go to Razorpay and never to us.

They act on our instructions for these purposes. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

The permissions the app asks for

  • Location — to show where you are on the map, to navigate, and to share your position with your group while you ride. Trekov does not ask for background location: it uses a foreground service, with a notification you can see, only while a live trip is running.
  • Camera — to take a photo to post. Only when you open the camera.
  • Microphone — push-to-talk on a group trip. Only while you hold the button. Voice goes directly between the riders' phones and is not recorded or stored; to connect them, the phones see each other's internet (IP) address, and Google's STUN service helps them find each other.
  • Notifications — invites, riders asking to join your trip, and alerts from a trip you are on.

You can refuse any of them, or take them back later in your phone's settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

Keeping and deleting

What you post stays until you delete it or delete your account. Deleting your account removes your profile, your photos and their files, your posts, reviews, likes, comments, saved places, your trips and your notification tokens, and your sign-in itself. It cannot be undone. You can do it in the app under You → Delete account, or follow the instructions here.

Backups are kept for a short period by our hosting provider and age out on their own. Records we must keep for tax or legal reasons — payment receipts, for example — are kept for as long as the law requires.

Children

Trekov is for people aged 18 and over, and we do not knowingly keep data about anyone younger. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Your choices

You can see and edit your profile in the app, export nothing you did not put in, and delete everything at any time. If you want a copy of what is held about you, or want something corrected, write to trekonvibes@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.
