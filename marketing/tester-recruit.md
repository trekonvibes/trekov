# Getting 9 more Play testers (2026-09-21)

Play wants 12 testers opted in, staying for 14 days in a row. We have 3.
Aim for **15**, not 12: a few people always forget to tap "Become a tester",
and dropping below 12 restarts the 14 days.

How someone becomes a tester (put this in every message):
1. They send their **Gmail address** (the one on their Android phone).
2. It gets added to the Trekov tester list in Play Console.
3. They open **https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.trekov.app**, tap
   **Become a tester**, then **Download it on Google Play**, and install.
4. They keep it installed for 14 days (they don't have to use it daily).

iPhone users can't count. Only Android phones.
