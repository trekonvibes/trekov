Privacy Policy

Effective [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]] · Trekov is operated by [[LEGAL_NAME]].

What we collect

  • Account: email, username, name, profile photo, and your password (stored hashed by our auth provider).
  • Content: places you add, photos, captions, reviews, saved places, trips, and business listings and products.
  • Location: used on your phone for the map and navigation. When you post a photo we store proof that it was taken on location (time, distance from the place, GPS accuracy) — not your exact coordinates. Live location in a group trip is shared only with that trip's members, and only while you have sharing on.
  • Device: a random device identifier, used to keep your account signed in on one device at a time.
  • Payments: processed by Razorpay. We keep the payment reference, plan, amount and dates — never card or bank details.

How we use it

To run the service, show your content to other users as you choose, keep your account secure, process payments, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Service providers that run Trekov for us: Supabase (database and storage), Google Maps Platform (maps, places, routes), Razorpay (payments), and our email provider. They process data only on our instructions.

Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete your content, turn off live location sharing, and ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data by writing to trekonvibes@gmail.com.
