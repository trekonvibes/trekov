# Trekov policy pages — DRAFTS, not yet published

Razorpay checks these pages on the website before activating payments:
Pricing, Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Cancellation & Refund Policy,
Contact Us (and Shipping/Delivery, which for Trekov is "digital, instant").

Fill every `[[...]]` placeholder with the real details (they must match what
you give Razorpay), then have them reviewed — these are plain-language drafts,
not legal advice. Once filled, they go to `public/<name>/index.html` so they
live at trekov.com/pricing/, /terms/, /privacy/, /refunds/, /contact/, and the
landing page footer links to them.

Placeholders:
- [[LEGAL_NAME]]     — the business or person Razorpay pays (e.g. a proprietorship or company name)
- [[ADDRESS]]        — registered address
- [[PHONE]]          — support phone number
- [[HOURS]]          — support hours, e.g. "Mon–Sat, 10:00–18:00 IST"
- [[GRIEVANCE_NAME]] — grievance officer's name (IT Rules / DPDP Act)
- [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]] — the date the policies take effect
