How payment works

Prices are in Indian rupees and include applicable taxes unless stated otherwise. You pay once for a year through Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets). Your plan starts as soon as the payment is confirmed and lasts one year from that day; paying again before it ends adds another year. Plans do not renew automatically.

Delivery

Trekov is a digital service. Nothing is shipped: your plan is activated on your account immediately after payment.

Accounts created before paid plans were introduced keep their access free.
