How it works

Plan it. Bring the crew.
Ride it live.

Plan the route

Build a trip from real places, in the order you will ride them. Trekov gives the distance and time between every stop — and the fuel, food, stays and tolls along the way.

Bring your crew

Invite riders by their handle, or open the ride so others can find it and ask to join. You approve everyone, and pick a captain to lead.

Ride it live

Everyone on one map with turn-by-turn navigation, one-tap alerts and push-to-talk — and the map downloaded for the stretches with no signal.

The host runs the ride

Nine on the road.
One list, one captain.

Whoever creates the trip is the host. Everyone else joins because the host said yes — so the live map only ever shows the people you are actually riding with.

  • Invite riders by their handle, or share the trip as a link that opens even without the app.
  • Open the ride so other riders can find it, then approve each one who asks to join.
  • Name a captain for the group to follow — yourself, or any rider on the trip.
  • See who is sharing their position, and hide yours from one person while the rest still see you.

Built for the road

Everything a group ride needs,
on one screen.

A group chat full of "where are you?" does not work at 60 km/h. Trekov puts the route, the riders and the conversation on the map you are already looking at.

Everyone on one map

See every rider live — where they are, which way they are heading and how fast — with the whole route drawn under them.

STOP · WAIT · LET'S GO

One tap and every phone in the group flashes full screen, buzzes and sounds a loud alarm — made to cut through wind and a helmet.

STOP WAIT GO

Push-to-talk

Hold the button and say it to the whole group. Nothing is sent unless you are holding it, and nothing is recorded.

Open rides New

Make a group trip public and other riders can find it and ask to join. You approve each one — until then they see only the title, the dates and where it starts and ends.

A host and a captain

Whoever creates the ride runs it: adds and removes riders, and names a captain for the group to follow. A rider asking to join reaches the host's phone even with Trekov closed.

Navigation that stays on

Turn-by-turn with the group still on the map. The screen stays awake, it warns you when you leave the route, and a dropped signal never wipes the line you are following.

Maps that work offline

Download the route before you go, so the map and your position still work in the valleys where the network gives up.

Find where to go

Stays, food, fuel, garages, rentals and things to see near you or your route — with photos from riders who were actually there, and their profiles one tap away.

Your location, your rules

Your live position goes only to the people on your trip, and is never stored on our servers. Switch sharing off, or hide from one rider while the rest still see you.
