How it works

From a pin on a map
to a trip you can send.

Zoom in

Pan anywhere on the map. Every pin is a real place, sized by how many people have shot there.

Open the place

Recent photos first, with the season to go, what to know before you do, and a map link.

Plan it, send it

Drop places into a trip, order the stops, add notes — then share the whole itinerary as a link.

Why not just use Instagram

Because a saved post isn't a plan.

Screenshots and saved folders are where travel inspiration goes to die. Trekov is organised the way you actually search — by where, not by who.

Geography, not a timeline

Photos live on the map where they were taken. Zoom into a valley and see everything anyone has shot in it.

Seasons, not just scenery

Every place carries its window. Half the country is spectacular in October and shut in January — the list knows which.

Trips you can hand over

Build an itinerary from the map, then share it as a link. Whoever opens it gets the stops, the notes and the dates.

A list you'll actually open

Saved places group by country, keep their best season, and link straight to Maps. Not a folder of 400 unlabelled screenshots.
